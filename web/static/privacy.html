Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we store

  • Google profile basics — your name, email, and avatar, used only to sign you in.
  • Your configuration — Telegram API credentials, channel list, checkvisaslots.com key, and your Telegram session file, used solely to run your monitor.
  • Event history — alerts sent, poll results, and channel-message previews, shown on your dashboard.

What we don't do

  • We never read your personal Telegram chats — only the public channels you configure.
  • We never send messages from your Telegram account.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Third parties

Channel messages you monitor are sent to a cloud AI provider (Groq) for classification. Alerts are delivered via Telegram's Bot API. Slot data comes from checkvisaslots.com using your own key.

Deletion

Want your account and all data removed? Contact the operator (or the email on the sign-in page) and everything — config, sessions, history — is deleted.
